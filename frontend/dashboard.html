        A little guidance goes a long way.

        Here’s how an agreement moves through your workspace.

        What is a client file?

        A client file represents one job or case for one client. It sits under a saved client and contains one or more agreements. Each agreement has its own signing request, status and final records.

        How do agreement templates work?

        Upload the agreement your company uses, review every page, place fields, and assign signer roles. Once a version is published, it can be reused. Editing a template does not change an agreement already issued.

        Does creating a file send the agreement?

        No. Confirm the document, signer details, and company values first. A separate Send to client action sends it after those checks pass.

        How do client invitations work?

        Sending an agreement creates a private signing link and an access code for the client. Both work for 24 hours and can be used more than once in that time. In this demo they open in a pop-up for you to share, instead of being emailed. The client must enter the code before viewing the agreement.

        When is a file ready to start?

        The client signs first. If required, the company countersigns next. Each agreement completes after its required signatures, PDF and history are saved. A job is ready to start when all of its agreements are completed.

        What can I use now?

        Prepare PDF or DOCX templates, save client files, and send signing invitations. Clients sign first; companies countersign when required. Download completed documents, or cancel an unfinished request and explicitly prepare a replacement. Earlier signing records stay in the file’s history.

        Need help with your account?

        Ask your company administrator for help with access or a password reset.
