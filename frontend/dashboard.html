      A little guidance goes a long way.

      Here’s how an agreement moves through your workspace.

      What is a client file?

      A client file represents one job or case for one client. It brings together client details, its agreement request, and the signing history.

      How do agreement templates work?

      Upload the agreement your company uses, review every page, place fields, and assign signer roles. Once a version is published, it can be reused. Editing a template does not change an agreement already issued.

      Does creating a file send the agreement?

      No. Confirm the document, signer details, and company values first. A separate Send request — Demo action creates the request after those checks pass.

      How will demo invitations work?

      No email is sent. The company view will show the signing link, access code, expiry countdown, and Open/Copy controls below the send button. The client must verify the code before viewing the agreement.

      When is a file ready to start?

      The client signs first. If required, the company countersigns next. The file becomes ready only after all required signatures are confirmed and both the completed PDF and signing history have been saved.

      What can I use now?

      Create, save, reopen, and update client-file drafts. Search and filter your company’s saved files, or log out. Upload and preview PDF or DOCX agreements, prepare fields, publish reusable versions, and select a fixed version for a client-file draft. Saving a draft does not send an invitation or record a signature.
